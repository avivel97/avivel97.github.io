Classes of work

How I can contribute

Engagements can be advisory-only or extend through analysis, implementation, and handover.

01

Consulting & Problem Framing

I help formulate the real business problem, test initial assumptions, identify data constraints, and determine the most rational path to a solution.

  • Analytical briefs for management
  • Problem and hypothesis framing sessions
  • Independent expert review of analytical initiatives
  • Research plan and measurement system design
02

Market & Business Analysis

I study markets, categories, customers, and the competitive environment to turn data into a clear foundation for commercial decisions.

  • Market potential and growth opportunity assessment
  • Category, assortment, and customer base analysis
  • Segmentation and market structure analysis
  • Scenario modeling and sensitivity analysis
03

Analytical Solution Development

I turn analytical approaches into practical tools and models that the business can use every day without an analyst.

  • Forecasting and prioritization models
  • Decision-support system prototypes
  • Analytical process automation
  • Documentation and team handover
04

Research Design & Evaluation

I design studies and experiments that produce reliable conclusions and separate real effects from random observations.

  • Experiment and pilot design
  • Methodology and model validation
  • KPI and outcome measurement system design
  • Findings and analytical reporting
