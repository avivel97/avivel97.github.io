Illustrative case studies

What an engagement could look like

These are representative scopes, not claims about completed client projects.

Case 01 Strategy + analysis

New Product Market Entrance

Should a new product enter this market, through which segment, and under what conditions?

Size reachable demand, map customer segments and competitors, identify entry barriers, compare channel options, and test price-volume scenarios. The final recommendation includes explicit go, conditional-go, and stop criteria.

Outputs: market map, scenario model, risk register, decision brief, 90-day validation plan.

Selected references

  1. OECD, Competition Assessment Toolkit
  2. Cooper, Stage-Gate Idea-to-Launch Process
Case 02 Retail + optimization

Category Management Optimization

Which products deserve space, where, and with what role in the category?

Combine sales, margin, availability, promotion, and substitution signals. Segment stores, clarify SKU roles, identify duplication and gaps, and propose listing, delisting, and range rules with safeguards for customer choice.

Outputs: category diagnostic, store clusters, assortment scenarios, KPI framework, implementation backlog.

Selected references

  1. GS1, Category Management for Customer Needs
  2. Kok, Fisher & Vaidyanathan, Assortment Planning
Case 03 Forecasting + operations

Demand Forecasting & Inventory Decisions

How much should the business expect to sell, and which exceptions need human attention?

Audit demand data, establish transparent baselines, account for promotions and seasonality, compare forecast accuracy at useful business levels, and connect forecasts to replenishment or capacity scenarios.

Outputs: forecast benchmark, exception rules, scenario dashboard, operating recommendations, model documentation.

Selected references

  1. Fildes, Ma & Kolassa, Retail Forecasting
  2. Hyndman & Athanasopoulos, Forecasting: Principles and Practice
Case 04 Decision intelligence

From BI Dashboards to Decisions

How can reporting be connected to ownership, action, and measurable business outcomes?

Map recurring decisions, their owners, inputs, constraints, and action thresholds. Identify where a dashboard is sufficient, where a recommendation layer adds value, and where human judgment must remain explicit.

Outputs: decision map, KPI-to-action logic, prototype specification, governance model, adoption plan.

Selected references

  1. Delen & Demirkan, Data, Information and Analytics as Services
  2. Shrestha, Ben-Menahem & von Krogh, AI Decision Structures
